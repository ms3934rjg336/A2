Our Mission

Building bridges between developers and community members

Empowering Communication at Halletts Point

At the Halletts Point Community Planning organization, we believe that effective communication between developers and residents is the cornerstone of thriving communities. Located in the heart of Astoria, Queens, Halletts Point represents a unique opportunity to create a model for urban development that truly serves all stakeholders.

For too long, community members and developers have struggled to find common ground. Residents feel unheard in the planning process, while developers lack clear channels to understand community needs and concerns. This disconnect leads to projects that miss the mark, strained relationships, and missed opportunities for creating spaces that everyone can be proud of.

What We Do

Our platform provides a digital space where developers and community members can collaborate in real-time. Through interactive tools, visual feedback systems, and structured dialogue, we're breaking down barriers and building understanding.

  • Visual Annotation Tools: Community members can directly mark up development plans, adding notes and suggestions exactly where they matter most.
  • Developer Portal: Developers receive organized, actionable feedback that helps them understand community priorities and concerns.
  • Transparent Process: All feedback is documented and tracked, ensuring accountability and creating a historical record of community input.
  • Accessibility: Our platform is designed to be accessible to all community members, regardless of technical expertise.

Our Vision for Astoria

We envision a Halletts Point where every resident has a voice in shaping their neighborhood's future. Where developers have the resources and community insights needed to create spaces that genuinely serve local needs. Where planning meetings are productive conversations rather than confrontations.

By providing better tools for communication, we're not just improving individual projects—we're building a stronger, more connected community. Every annotation, every comment, every conversation brings us closer to a Halletts Point that works for everyone.

Join Us

Whether you're a long-time resident, a new community member, or a developer working in the area, your voice matters. Join us in creating a more collaborative, transparent, and effective approach to community planning at Halletts Point.

Together, we're not just building buildings—we're building community.
